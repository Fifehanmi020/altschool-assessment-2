FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

No. You can shorten links without an account, but signing up unlocks custom URLs, link history, QR codes and analytics.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

Links created with an account stay active as long as your account is in good standing. You can also set an expiry date on any link.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

The Basic plan includes unlimited URL shortening. Higher plans add advanced analytics, branded domains and team features.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes. Use custom slugs and aliases on any plan, and connect your own branded domain on the Professional and Teams plans.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes. Every link comes with click analytics, including totals, referrers, locations and devices.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

All links are served over HTTPS and scanned against known malicious and spam sources before they go live.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code is a scannable image that opens your link instantly on a phone camera. Use it on posters, packaging, menus and event materials.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

Yes. API access is included in the Teams plan, so you can create and manage links straight from your own apps.
